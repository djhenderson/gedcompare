# a collection of sample GEDCOM files.

These files have been collected from many sources of the last 40+ years.

I have used them to explore the features of genealogy programs, including
commercial programs, commercial trial versions, shareware, freeware,
and programs I have written myself.

smallest551.ged is the smallest "legal" GEDCOM 5.5.1 file.

smallest551_AQ16.ged was made by importing the above, adding one person,
exporting the GEDCOM, and deleting the one person.
